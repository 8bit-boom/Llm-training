# Step 2 · Make training data

> **Not available in this version yet.** This page shows what the step will do.

## What this step does
Turns your world into **practice questions and answers** the model learns from.

## What you will do
Not available in this version yet. In the finished program you choose which kinds of examples to make (simple facts, deeper questions, rules practice, NPC role-play, campaign history, 'that is not in the lore'), optionally let a helper model write them, review them, and save a version.

## What happens next
A saved version of your training data.
