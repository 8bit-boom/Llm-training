# Welcome to ND Lore Forge

This program teaches a small AI model **your nd-world world**: its lore, its rules and its mechanics. The result is a model
that knows your world by heart and that you can use in nd-world instead of a big general model.

## What this step does
Lets you create a **project** (one project = one world) or open one you made before.

## What you need before you start
- An nd-world world you want the model to learn.
- Later: an NVIDIA graphics card (your RTX 5090 is ideal). You do not need it for the first steps.

## What to do
1. Click **Create a new project**.
2. Type a name, for example the name of your world, and choose **Create**.
3. The program takes you to **Step 1 · Get world data**.

## What happens next
You always work through five steps, top to bottom on the left. A step is greyed out until the one before it is done. Every
page has this help panel on the right; you can hide it with **Help panel** in the toolbar.

**Simple mode** (default) shows only the choices that matter. **Expert mode** (toolbar) shows every setting.
